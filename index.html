Tasting
Deep ruby. Notes of dark cherry, plum, cedar, graphite and earthy minerality. Long, silky finish.
Serving
Decant 2 hours before serving. Optimal serving temperature 16–18 °C. Use a large Bordeaux glass.
Pairings
Grilled Wagyu, duck confit, aged Comté, black truffle risotto, lamb rack with rosemary jus.
Cellaring
Store on its side at 12–14 °C, 70% humidity. Optimal drinking window: 2024–2040. Peak: 2026–2032.

Good to know

What is this page?

It’s the product’s digital passport: a record the brand created so you can check it’s genuine and see how and where it was made.

How do I know it’s authentic?

The badge at the top is checked against a secure record that can’t be edited or faked. If your item has a tap-to-verify tag, tapping it adds a second check.

Where do these details come from?

Straight from the brand and the partners who handled the product. Each step shown here was recorded by them.

Why do products have this?

Brands use it to prove an item is real and to share its story, from origin to sustainability. New EU rules are starting to require this kind of product information too.
